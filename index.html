Fan Yang 杨帆

I am a researcher in embodied AI and computer vision. I received my Ph.D. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Wenbing Tao. I also received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from HUST.

My research interests include 3D vision, point cloud registration, and embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in connecting geometric perception with robot learning and interaction with the physical world.

Research

I study how to learn reliable geometric representations and correspondences for understanding the 3D world. My work spans robust point cloud registration, correspondence learning, and visual geometry, with a growing focus on embodied AI and world models.

Zhi Chen, K. Sun, Fan Yang, Lin Guo, Wenbing Tao

IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI), 2023

paper  /  code

Second-order spatial compatibility improves the generation of reliable registration hypotheses. A feature- and spatial-consistency-aware truncated Chamfer distance selects the final transformation, including when the initial correspondence set contains very few inliers.

SC²-PCR: A Second Order Spatial Compatibility for Efficient and Robust Point Cloud Registration

Zhi Chen, K. Sun, Fan Yang, Wenbing Tao

CVPR, 2022

paper  /  code

A second-order spatial compatibility measure identifies mutually consistent correspondences despite substantial outlier contamination. Reliable seed selection, two-stage consensus sampling, and weighted SVD produce robust rigid registration hypotheses.

DetarNet: Decoupling Translation and Rotation by Siamese Network for Point Cloud Registration

Zhi Chen, Fan Yang, Wenbing Tao

AAAI, 2022 · Oral

paper  /  code

A Siamese network decouples translation and rotation estimation to reduce their mutual interference. Progressive feature drift recovers translation, while consensus encoding and spatial-channel attention identify reliable correspondences for rotation estimation.

Cascade network with guided loss and hybrid attention for finding good correspondences

Zhi Chen, Fan Yang, Wenbing Tao

AAAI 35(2), 1123–1131, 2021

paper  /  code

Guided Loss dynamically adjusts the training objective using the F-measure to address imbalanced inlier and outlier correspondences. Hybrid attention and a cascade architecture progressively refine correspondence classification and relative pose estimation.
