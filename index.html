Fan Yang

I am currently a researcher at RoboScience. I received my Ph.D. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I was advised by Prof. Wenbing Tao in the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation. I also received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from HUST.

My current research focuses on embodied AI and world models, aiming to build intelligent agents that understand, predict, and act in the physical world. My previous research explored 3D vision and geometric learning.
