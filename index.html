Fan Yang

I am currently a researcher in embodied AI and world models at RoboScience. I received my Ph.D. from the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Wenbing Tao. I also received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from HUST.

My research interests lie in 3D vision and embodied intelligence. I develop geometric representations and correspondence learning methods for understanding the 3D world, and am increasingly interested in world models that connect visual perception with robot learning and physical interaction.
